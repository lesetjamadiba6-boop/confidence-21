A LETTER FOR YOU

Dear Confidence,

Happy 21st birthday.

It is genuinely a little crazy to think that we have known each other since 2019. We have gone from being younger versions of ourselves, still figuring life out, to watching each other grow into the people we are becoming now.

Somewhere along the way, you stopped feeling like just a friend and became family. You are genuinely like a sister to me — the kind of person I can care for, laugh with, annoy, support, and still know that underneath everything, the friendship is real.

I admire the way you carry yourself. There is a softness about you, but there is strength there too. You are kind, humble and thoughtful, and you have this calm presence even when life itself is doing the absolute most.

I have also had the privilege of seeing you grow. Not only physically — because obviously the school-uniform era had to end eventually 😂 — but in your maturity, your character, your confidence and the way you carry yourself.

As you turn 21, I hope you never feel pressured to have everything figured out. Twenty-one is not a deadline. It is simply another chapter.

I hope this chapter brings you peace — real peace. I hope you experience friendships that feel safe, opportunities that stretch you, moments that make you laugh until your stomach hurts, and achievements that remind you just how capable you are.

I hope you remain soft without letting the world take advantage of your softness. I hope you remain kind while learning when to choose yourself. And I hope you keep becoming more confident in who you are — which, considering your name, feels like something you were contractually obligated to do anyway.

Most importantly, I hope you always remember that you are loved. Not because of what you achieve, how you look, what you can give people, or how well life is going — simply because you are you.

Thank you for every year of friendship since 2019. Thank you for the memories we already have and for all the random moments that somehow became important without us even noticing.

I do not know exactly where life will take either of us, but I genuinely hope that years from now we will still be able to look back at these days, laugh at ourselves and say, “Wow… we really grew up.”

Happy birthday, Confidence. Welcome to Chapter 21. 🤍

Always rooting for you,
Lesetja
